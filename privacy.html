Privacy Policy

Application: KQ-AI Automation

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Purpose

KQ-AI Automation is an internal task automation system that uses rclone to synchronize task queue files with Google Drive.

2. Google Drive Data

The application accesses Google Drive only after authorization by the Google account owner. Depending on the granted permissions, it may read, create, update, and synchronize files required for task automation.

3. Data Usage

Google Drive data is used for authorized internal task execution, synchronization, monitoring, and troubleshooting. It is not used for advertising or sold.

4. Storage and Retention

Task files and operational records may be retained in authorized Google Drive locations and local systems for operational and audit purposes until removed by the operator.

OAuth credentials are maintained in the operator's local rclone configuration and are not collected by this public website.

5. Data Sharing

Google Drive data is not published through this website. Access is limited to authorized internal workflows and any services explicitly configured by the operator for task processing.

6. Security

The operator is responsible for restricting access to local credentials, task files, and operational records.

7. User Control

The account owner can revoke application access through Google Account security settings and request deletion of locally retained data.
